The lantern

The object on its own, every side. Drag to turn it; it slowly turns by itself when left alone. Use the switches to light it, open the back and pull the drives, or see through the concrete. When it looks right, make the files: cut files for the shops, and print files for the glass in the colors you chose.

What you're seeing

  • Glass: 17 panes of 6 mm cast acrylic, 210 × 220 mm, 6 mm gaps, open top. Each pane steps darker toward the center.
  • Film: a soft-edged square of color printed on each pane, growing toward the center, where it's just bigger than the window.
  • Window: notched out of the middle 7 panes, 158 × 154 mm, around the computer.
  • Rods: four M5 stainless rods with acorn nuts hold the panes as one block, on clear acrylic spacer rings.
  • Computer: MINIX Z100-0dB, fanless, 123 × 120 × 46 mm, standing on edge.
  • Base: cast GFRC concrete, 210 × 200 × 172 mm, on an inset felt plinth.
  • Top: 8 mm aluminum plate with air slots under the pane gaps, LED strips under the pane edges and a grommet for the computer's cables.
  • Back: 2 mm aluminum plate set flush in a cast rebate on four thumbscrews. Air slots, power inlet, HDMI and Ethernet across the top, where the air space is; a window onto the peak-hold label and a vent plug below.
  • Cables: right-angle plugs point down, so the three cables drop straight down the back.
  • Vault: two 3.5" drives standing in a sled on elastic cords, on plastic runners; it slides out the back.
  • Power: one 12 V supply in the air space under the top plate runs the computer, drives and lights. One cord.
  • Button: a flush steel button on the front: tap for on and off, double-tap for the next mode, hold for lamp.
