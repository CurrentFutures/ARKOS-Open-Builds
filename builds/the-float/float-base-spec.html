What it is

A closed, hollow walnut box, 303 × 464 × 90 mm, that sits on a desk and carries an Antec C8 Curve Wood PC case (about 15 kg loaded) on four 22 mm brass posts. The case floats 40 mm above the base; that gap is the PC's air intake, so the base has no vents. The top is visible through the gap and lit from above, so it's a show surface. The base also houses a brass toggle switch, a small electronics board, LED strips, and a brass inlay line with a thin lit reveal beneath it.

Supplied by the owner

  • Brass flat strip, about 1.5 × 6 mm, ~80 cm total

  • LED strips and the circuit board (fitted later by the owner)
  • Optional: the walnut-veneer ray panel (later)

Questions for the woodworker

  • Solid walnut or veneer ply, and the price difference?
  • Can you set the brass inlay, or would you rather we do it after?
  • Preferred finish and how it ages next to brass?
  • Lead time? (The case arrives around early December; final measurements follow.)
  • Would you cut the ray panel too, or should a laser shop?

Verify before cutting

  1. Case footprint and the positions of its foot screw holes (the posts bolt into them).
  2. PSU power socket position, which sets the cord clip location.
  3. Toggle bushing diameter and thread length.
  4. That the case's bottom dust filter slides out with the posts in place.
