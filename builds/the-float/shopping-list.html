Custom work: who can make it

Walnut base (box, brass inlay channel, toggle and ray pockets, post blocking)

Who: Makerspace laser/CNC (cheapest, you learn), a custom woodworker on Etsy, or an online CNC/plywood shop (first · second)

Send the drawing; ask for walnut veneer ply over a birch frame, a 6 mm channel for the brass, and a removable bottom. Etsy walnut-riser makers already sell custom widths ($90–230 for standard risers); a custom base with inlay will cost more. $150–500 est.

Brass posts (4, one drilled through for wires)

Who: Xometry (upload a simple CAD file, choose brass 360, instant quote) or any local machine shop (link)

Spec: 7/8" (22 mm) dia × 40 mm, faces square, M6 or 1/4-20 tapped both ends, one with a 3/8" through-hole. A local shop can do this from the 7/8" bar in under an hour. $60–250 est.

Dead-front ray panel (laser-drilled walnut veneer)

Who: Makerspace laser, Ponoko (laser wood), or Cut Laser Cut (walnut-veneer MDF) (first · second)

Test hole size on scrap first; veneer can splinter. $20–60 est.

Optional perforated brass back plate (plan B / North XL)

Who: SendCutSend, 1 mm (0.040") 260 brass (link)

Only for the North XL version. $100–300 est.
