C8 Float Study
House build · Antec C8 Curve Wood version

The float

The C8's curved glass wrapped in one reeded panel, floating 4 cm above a solid walnut block on four brass posts. Bottom fans breathe through the gap and light it. One post is a hollow tube carrying the switch wires up into the case.

What changed from the North XL version

The light now comes mostly from ten programmable fans (three under the card, three on the side, three on the radiator, one at the back). Their color is yours to set; gold is the default. Edge strips stay as warm fill. The base no longer breathes, so it has no holes: the gap does the airflow. The walnut is the C8's own side panel plus the base.

Antec C8 Curve Wood: 303 × 464 × 476 mm (W × D × H), from Antec's listing. Brass posts 4 cm. Parts are generic shapes at real sizes. Top radiator support on the Curve version should be confirmed before buying a 360 mm cooler.
