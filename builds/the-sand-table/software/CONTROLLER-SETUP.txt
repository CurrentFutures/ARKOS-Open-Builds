CONTROLLER SETUP: Raspberry Pi Pico in the tray

1. Arduino IDE (https://www.arduino.cc/en/software).
   File > Preferences > Additional boards manager URLs:
     https://github.com/earlephilhower/arduino-pico/releases/download/global/package_rp2040_index.json
   Tools > Board > Boards Manager: install "Raspberry Pi Pico/RP2040" (Earle Philhower).
   Tools > Board: "Raspberry Pi Pico" (not Pico W).

2. Open sandtable-controller/sandtable-controller.ino.
   Before the first upload, check MAX_DRIVE against your desk-test notes (start low: 0.3).
   Hold BOOTSEL on the Pico, plug in USB, Upload.

3. Choose your figure (the setup ritual, about 20 minutes, in a quiet room)
   Sand on the plate, glass off, everyone watching.
   - Press SETUP once. The plate slowly sweeps from low notes to high and shows every
     figure it can make, one after another.
   - When you see one you like, press SETUP. It now searches just around that note,
     back and forth, slowly.
   - Press SETUP again at the moment the figure is crispest. That note is saved; the
     plate rings once to confirm. That is your figure from now on.
   - Hold SETUP for 3 seconds at any time to cancel.
   Or by laptop over the Pico's USB (Arduino Serial Monitor, 115200, newline):
     f 312.5     hold the plate at 312.5 Hz
     save 312.5  make it your figure
     test 90     play a verdict at 90 (settled); try 30 to see unsettled
     thresh 50   below this a task also counts on the UNSETTLED counter
     show        print the saved settings

4. The counters only ever count up. Nothing in the firmware can turn them back.
